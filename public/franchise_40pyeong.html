Executive Investment Snapshot

A 40-pyeong branch is designed for dine-in, takeout and delivery-ready operations in mall or high-foot-traffic locations. The largest cash requirement usually comes before opening day.

Complete Store Footprint

Dining area, service counter, kitchen line, cold and dry storage, staff area and utility zones.

Primary Cost Drivers

Fit-out, kitchen equipment and other setup expenses carry the heaviest pre-opening capital requirement.

Capital Buffer Logic

Extra allowance protects against construction revisions, permit timing, supplier changes and landlord requirements.

Opening Readiness Budget

These costs prepare the store to open legally, operate smoothly and launch with demand.

  • Franchise fee / brand rights: ₱1.0M – ₱3.0M
  • Permits and legal: ₱300K – ₱900K
  • Opening inventory: ₱1.0M – ₱2.2M
  • Training and pre-opening: ₱600K – ₱1.5M
  • Marketing and launch: ₱700K – ₱2.0M
  • Other setup expenses: ₱3.5M – ₱8.0M

Store Systems

The front-of-house identity and operating rhythm depend on reliable furniture, signage, POS, kitchen display, CCTV, internet and delivery integration.

Investor emphasis: These categories are smaller than fit-out and kitchen equipment, but they strongly influence customer perception, order flow and sales reporting control.

Suggested Capital Preparation Scenarios

Choose capital planning based on site quality, landlord demands and desired safety margin.

Minimum Working Estimate

₱23.5M – ₱30M

Basic branch preparation with limited buffer. Suitable only when site condition and quotes are very clear.

Standard Mall / Commercial

₱30M – ₱40M

Recommended for typical mall or commercial spaces with normal deposits and standard fit-out requirements.

Prime Mall / Flagship

₱40M – ₱50M

Better for high-traffic malls, premium visibility, stricter landlord rules or stronger launch marketing.

Larger Buffer / Premium Site

₱50M+

Most conservative preparation for expensive sites, imported equipment, longer lead times and heavier contingency.

Verification Checklist & Next Steps

Before presenting the amount as final, confirm every major cost line with official documents.

1

Franchise Agreement

Confirm franchise fee, package inclusions, territory rules, royalties and brand obligations.

2

Supplier Quotations

Secure kitchen equipment, signage, POS, furniture and opening inventory quotes.

3

Lease Terms

Review rent advance, deposits, construction bond, design rules, utilities and turnover condition.

4

Construction Bid

Validate fit-out through plans, engineering scope, material specs and contractor pricing.

5

LGU Compliance

Confirm business permits, sanitary, fire safety, occupancy and city-specific requirements.
